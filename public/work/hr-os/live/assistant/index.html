HR Assistant

Ask about your leave, pay, attendance and policies

What you can ask

This uses keyword matching, not AI. It looks up answers from actual company data.

Type your question naturally. The assistant looks for keywords and finds matching answers from:

  • Your leave: "How many leaves do I have?" or "casual leave left"
  • Your payslip: "What was I paid?" or "payslip for July 2026"
  • Your attendance: "My attendance" or "was I marked late on the 15th"
  • Company holidays: "Holiday list" or "when is the next holiday"
  • Reimbursements: "How do I claim travel?" or "status of my claim"
  • Company policies: "Late coming policy" or "flexible working rules"

Questions it cannot answer are automatically sent to HR for a personal response. That's a feature, not a failure — some questions genuinely need a human.
